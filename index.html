About Ricky

From academic pressure to cinematic purpose.

For the past ten years, I have been wrestling with who I am and who I want to become. Growing up in a household that placed strong pressure on academics, I did not have many opportunities to creatively express myself. That changed when I randomly started making videos—filming my day-to-day life and experimenting with framing.

What started as curiosity slowly became something I could not let go of. During my time at university, I realized that filmmaking is what gives me purpose. I may have fully understood that a little late after graduating, but I am not giving up on this craft.

Independent Vision

As an emerging independent Asian American filmmaker, my mission is to craft stories that reflect authentic lived experiences, bridging cultural roots with modern cinematic expression.

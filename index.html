About Ricky Huynh

I am an independent filmmaker, director, and content creator based in the United States. My work centers on narrative storytelling, visual essays, and commercial videography that bridges intimate human emotion with clean, modern aesthetics.

Transitioning from academic pressures toward a full-time pursuit in cinema, my projects explore identity, family dynamics, and contemporary cultural nuances.

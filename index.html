About Me

Hi there! I'm Ricky.

I'm an independent filmmaker, freelance videographer, and editor based in Boston, with a love for storytelling in pretty much every form. I've been making videos for around 8 years now, and somewhere along the way, it grew from a hobby into something I genuinely want to build my life around.

These days, I work across event videography, directing, editing, social and digital content, and everything in between. I love capturing the energy of a live event, helping organizations and brands tell their stories, and bringing someone else's vision to life. At the same time, I'm constantly working on stories of my own through short films, social content, and other creative projects. Whether it's a client project or something I made just because I had an idea I couldn't stop thinking about, I'm always looking for new ways to create.

On the fun side, I like doing things that add a little plot to my story. I write and direct short films, watch a frankly ridiculous amount of movies, and seek out good Vietnamese food. Who doesn't like Vietnamese food?

Outside of filmmaking, I currently work as a Community Engagement Coordinator at VietAID Community Center, where I get to work directly with the community while continuing to pursue my creative career on the side.

Thanks for stopping by and taking a look at my work. Welcome to my world :D

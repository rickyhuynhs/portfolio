About Me

Hi there! I'm Ricky.

A recent college grad from Boston. I am a video content creator, freelance videographer, and editor with a passion for storytelling. With over 8 years of experience, I have developed expertise in videography, editing, and post-production. I work on crafting compelling video content for individuals, organizations, and companies, bringing their visions to life. My portfolio highlights the diverse and impactful projects I've worked on throughout my career.

On the fun side, I like doing things that add plot to my story. I write and direct short films, watch a crazy amount of movies, love Vietnamese food, and more. Who doesn't like more?

I'm currently working as a Community Engagement Coordinator at VietAID Community Center, while actively trying to pursue my dreams! I'm thrilled to share my work with you—thanks for stopping by!
